How a team comes into existenceand how skills come into existence inside it

Nothing here is hardcoded and nothing is central. A team is created the moment someone commits a G0, an administrative apex that carries that team's rules. From then on, everything born beneath it inherits those rules, and some of its skills inspect the others because they were written to and pointed at by an edge. A different team writes a different apex, sets different rules, and appoints different inspectors.

Some skills inspect the others.
Not a special class of thing, just an ordinary skill somebody wrote that other skills declared they would be inspected by. Remove the edge and it inspects nothing.

Two architecturesone is proven here in demo · one is proposed

The conceptual architecture is what this repository demonstrates: how a proposal reaches the record, what the gate refuses, and who signs. The reference deployment is how the same record would be operated inside an enterprise. It is deliberately not built here, because standing up a VPC in three days would have proved less about the governance than the rules did.
